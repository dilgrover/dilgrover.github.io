WHAT IT DOES

The countdownMonths, days, hours, minutes and seconds, in the film's style, with rolling digits.
Living picturesThe light breathes, mist drifts, dust hangs in the air, and the camera moves slowly.
Energy follows youMove along the buttons and green energy jumps to wherever you are. Leave it, and it powers up by itself.
SummonHold OK for two seconds: the room darkens, rings run across the floor, a flash.
Your own picturesFour slots, filled by link. Rotate goes through them every 1, 5 or 15 minutes.
Date, clock, weatherAlong the top, for your town.
All your appsOne press up from the buttons, in the order you choose.
ScreensaverThe countdown when the TV is idle (Google TV).

INSTALL IT (ABOUT 5 MINUTES)

It isn't in an app store, so you install it with a free app called Downloader. This is safe and normal on Google TV and Fire TV ("sideloading").

The link to type in Downloader

dilgrover.github.io/doom.apk

Or get it from the download page on GitHub (the .apk file), where the code is too.

GOOGLE TV · ANDROID TV

Chromecast with Google TV, Sony, TCL, Hisense…

  • Install Downloader (by AFTVnews) from the Play Store on the TV.
  • Turn on developer options: Settings → System → About, then press Android TV OS build 7 times.
  • Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources → allow Downloader.
  • Open Downloader, type dilgrover.github.io/doom.apk, press Go, then Install.
  • Open Doomsday from your apps. Delete the downloaded file when Downloader asks, to save space.
AMAZON FIRE TV

Fire TV Stick, Cube, Fire TV televisions

  • Search for Downloader in the Amazon Appstore and install it.
  • Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → Install unknown apps → turn on Downloader. No Developer options? My Fire TV → About, press your device's name 7 times, then go back.
  • Open Downloader, type dilgrover.github.io/doom.apk, press Go, then Install.
  • Open Doomsday from Your apps & games.

Make it your home screen: the first time you open it, it asks "Make Doomsday your home screen?" and shows you how. On Google TV it's one switch; Fire TV may not allow it.

THE PICTURE CODES FROM MY REEL

In the app, open Pictures, pick a slot's link box and press OK. The box already starts with https://tinyurl.com/, so you only type the code. Then press Download.

TIPS

Your own pictures: the link must open the picture itself, not a page around it. Wide (16:9) and large (1920×1080 or 4K) works best, darker on the left so the countdown reads well, with something bright in the middle to come alive: windows, torches, a glowing floor.

Make a short link at tinyurl.com and choose your own ending (for example my-hall). Then you only type my-hall in the app. Google Drive and Dropbox links need changing first: how to.

Apps row: press Up from the buttons. To reorder, hold OK on an app, move it, and OK to put it down.

Settings: clock (24 or 12 hour), weather and your town, Effects (Full or Calm), dim at night, a soft ticking sound, and the screensaver.

QUESTIONS

Is it really free? Yes. No account, no sign-in, no ads, nothing tracked. The code is open on GitHub.

Does it work on Fire TV? Yes. The screensaver and the Home button are Google TV only; Fire TV may not allow them.

"The site didn't let the TV download it"? Some sites block TVs. Use a Google Drive, Dropbox or direct image link, made short with tinyurl.

Does it go online? Only to download your pictures (when you press Download) and to read the weather for your town.

Updates? New versions go on the download page; the link above always has the latest I've tested.
